The second line compresses high-fidelity physical fields into **latent world models**. DMD and Koopman operators provide next-state prediction; PPO and Dreamer-style model-based RL then act in that latent space.

On the control side, sparse flow probes and distributed piezoelectric sensing are fused through temporal encoders and cross-attention. Learned gain networks design whole-body sensor–actuator topologies for continuum systems, with policy transfer from simulation to hardware.

This line connects PhD multiphysics control to later work on **embodied foundation models** — simulation-based post-training of VLA and world-model policies.
