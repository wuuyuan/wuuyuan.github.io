Our paper *[Nonlinear flapping and symmetry-breaking bifurcation modulation of a piezoelectric metamaterial beam in viscous flow](https://doi.org/10.1017/jfm.2025.10556)* is now published in *Journal of Fluid Mechanics* (vol. 1019, A2).

The work develops a strongly coupled fluid–structure–electro-control model and a self-sensing actuation strategy for modulating nonlinear flapping and symmetry-breaking bifurcations.
