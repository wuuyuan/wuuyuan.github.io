[Genie Sim 4.0](/projects/genie-sim/) is out. The technical report is *[Genie Sim: Towards the Next Generation of Embodied Intelligence](https://agibot-world.com/genie-sim/report/)*, signed by the GenieSim Team. I was part of that team while interning at AgiBot.

The release keeps simulation, evaluation, and model improvement on one loop. It covers RL post-training of robot foundation models, physics-simulation engines, and the RL infrastructure that runs rollout and training together. It also reports gains in foundation-model capability and in fine manipulation. Those capability gains are team results.

My work was simulation-based RL post-training, the physics simulation used for that training, and the RL infrastructure for rollout, inference, and multi-node training. The project page on this site collects the official links: the [project](https://agibot-world.com/genie-sim), the [code](https://github.com/AgibotTech/genie_sim), and the [technical report](https://agibot-world.com/genie-sim/report/). A longer note is in the [blog post](/posts/genie-sim-4/).
