abstract: "A control strategy is proposed for modulating nonlinear flapping and symmetry-breaking bifurcations of a piezoelectric metamaterial beam behind a circular cylinder in viscous flow. Distributed piezoelectric meta-cells with unidirectional circuits enable self-sensing and adaptive control. A strongly coupled fluid–structure–electro-control model in an ALE framework predicts large-deformation flapping, including limit-cycle, subharmonic, and symmetry-breaking regimes. Self-sensing actuation suppresses period-doubling, and Floquet analysis confirms delayed instability and enhanced flow-energy conversion."
