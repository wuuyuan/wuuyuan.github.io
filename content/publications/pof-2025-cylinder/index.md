abstract: "Synchronization-mode modulation and fluid–structure–acoustic responses are studied for a rigid circular cylinder on a nonlinear spring in viscous flow. A strongly coupled ALE FSI model with direct numerical simulation of compressible viscous flow identifies four sound-wave branches. Nonlinear stiffness modulates lock-in range, phase relationships, and transitions between in-phase and out-of-phase cylinder modes."
